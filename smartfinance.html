How Compound Interest Works

Compound interest is the interest on a loan or deposit calculated based on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods. It's often called "interest on interest" and can significantly boost your investments over the long term. The formula used is:

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)]

Where A = future value, P = initial principal, PMT = monthly contribution, r = annual interest rate, n = compounding frequency per year, t = time in years. Learn more about the mathematics of compound interest.

Tips for Smart Investing

  • Start early – Time is your biggest ally thanks to compounding.
  • Contribute regularly – Even small monthly amounts add up significantly.
  • Reinvest dividends – Automatically reinvesting dividends accelerates growth.
  • Keep fees low – High fees can eat into your returns. Compare brokers with NerdWallet.
  • Diversify – Spread investments across asset classes to manage risk.
